# Three-chamber passive flow

- **State:** `CONCEPT_PROPOSED`; no hydraulic or physical test.
- **Function:** route fluid through three connected regions under a defined driving head.
- **Alternatives:** open-top over/under baffles; enclosed low-to-high channels; parallel branches; modular connected chambers. These differ in air trapping, cleaning, leakage paths and head-loss behavior.
- **Selection conditions:** open-top paths are inspectable and vent readily but depend on orientation/free-surface levels. Enclosed paths constrain routing but can trap air and are harder to clean. A passive route requires adequate elevation/head; “passive circulation” without a specified source of pressure/head is not a complete operating requirement.
- **Interfaces:** inlet/outlet tubing, inter-chamber openings, removable cover/seal, fill/vent/drain points and installation orientation.
- **Space checks:** channel cross sections, wall/seal land, fluid level range, bubble escape, tool/hand access and cleanout path.
- **Verification:** CAD corridor checks establish sampled geometric clearance only. Flow rate, head loss, siphon/priming, air purge, leakage and fouling require analysis or physical test appropriate to use.
- **Risks:** disconnected or bypassed route, capillary/dead zones, trapped air, insufficient head, inaccessible deposits, unsealed service cover.
- **Evidence:** concept only; `NOT_EVALUATED` hydraulically.
