## Known limits and follow-up

- The built-in design review is sequential and reuses project evidence; it does not claim independent review. A fresh Codex context can challenge a design, but that process is not an independent engineer.
- Functional architecture, load path, fastening adequacy, retention force, fatigue and maintenance ergonomics still need engineering judgment or physical testing. The review cannot promote declared YAML prose to proof.
- Fluid path checks sample a declared swept corridor against B-rep solids. They do not prove watertight sealing, continuous clearance between samples, flow rate, pressure loss, priming, fouling behavior or CFD performance.
- Wall thickness is sampled and directional, not a complete global minimum. Orthogonal orientation/support analyses are heuristics.
- Orca estimates use explicit presets but do not prove the generated 3MF will be accepted by a specific printer firmware or produce a successful/strong part. No printer communication or printing was performed.
- Static section PNGs show tessellated intersection outlines without hatching; they are not a dimensioned drawing or a replacement for B-rep measurements.
- Next useful steps are calibrated print coupons for each real printer/nozzle/material/process, documented load/retention checks for selected joints, and physical flow/fit testing for products that use those functions.
