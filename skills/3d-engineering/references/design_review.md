# Critical design review

Run `cad3d build`, geometry architecture checks and `cad3d review`; inspect the generated findings, evidence, severity, impact, correction and required verification. Challenge: function assigned to every part; support/retention for every installed part; complete interfaces; assembly and maintenance access; service path; commercial-part removal; dimensions derived from assumptions; fit tolerance source; physical route continuity; unsupported claims; residual risks; and stale or absent evidence.

Critical findings block approval. Do not turn checklist completion into PASS. Separate checks a program executes from engineering judgment. The built-in review is sequential and shares project evidence with the author; it is not independent. Where supported, conduct a fresh-context adversarial review and preserve its findings. Explicitly list unanswered questions and physical validation still needed.
